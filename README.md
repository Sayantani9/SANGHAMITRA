## About

**WHY YZ?** is an initiative by Sanghamitra Ventures focused on connecting ambitious students with structured learning, practical projects, and opportunities to demonstrate their capabilities.

We believe an internship should be more than a certificate or another résumé entry.

It should create something tangible: work you can explain, demonstrate, improve, and own.

Our approach combines structured mentorship, practical assignments, execution milestones, reviews, and performance-based progression.

## What We Aim to Build

- Practical, portfolio-oriented learning experiences.
- Strong technical and professional foundations.
- Exposure to real-world workflows and project execution.
- Better communication, collaboration, and accountability.
- A culture of curiosity, innovation, and continuous improvement.

## Selection & Evaluation Process

Our internship programme follows a structured, multi-stage selection process.

**Gate 00 — Opening Workshop**
- One-day opening workshop.
- Registration fee: ₹100.
- Payment proof must be submitted with the application.
- Registration does not guarantee selection.

**Level 01 — 15 Days**
- Assigned workflows and milestones.
- Practical execution and deliverables.
- Reviews and timeline adherence.
- Completion-based evaluation.

**Gate 02 — Interview**
- Performance review.
- Interview and assessment.
- Selection for the next stage.

**Level 02 — 30 Days**
- Advanced responsibilities.
- Greater project ownership.
- Consistent delivery.
- Final demonstration of work.

Progression and refund eligibility are governed by the programme's published criteria and applicable policies.

## Our Philosophy

We don't measure potential by how impressive a résumé looks.

We look for:

- Curiosity over complacency.
- Execution over empty promises.
- Consistency over temporary enthusiasm.
- Collaboration over individual recognition.
- Demonstrable work over unsupported claims.

**Be serious. Be curious. Ship the work.**
